Chitkara University

Chitkara University is a leading private, multi-disciplinary institution located in Jansla Village along the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway (NH-64), roughly 30 km from Chandigarh. Established in 2010 by the Punjab State Legislature, the university holds an NAAC A+ accreditation and offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, business management, healthcare, pharmacy, architecture, and design. Known for its strong industry tie-ups with global giants like NVIDIA and SAP, a vibrant modern campus, and active placement records, Chitkara blends academic rigor with practical, research-oriented learning.
